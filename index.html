Information stored on your device

NutriLens stores app data locally on your device, including:

  • Meal and water logs
  • Favorites
  • Nutrition goals
  • Reminder settings
  • Allergen preferences
  • Cached packaged-food results

NutriLens does not require you to create an account, and we do not operate a NutriLens cloud service for this information.

If you export your history or create a backup, the resulting file is saved or shared using the options you select on your device. A backup can include your logs, settings, favorites, and cached product records. Restoring a backup replaces data currently stored in the app.

Camera and food photos

NutriLens requests camera access when you choose to scan a barcode. If you select a food photo for analysis, the image is analyzed on your device using Google ML Kit image labeling. NutriLens does not upload the photo to a NutriLens server.

Google Play services may download the ML Kit model. Google's handling of information related to its services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Barcode lookups

When you look up a packaged food by barcode, NutriLens sends the barcode to the Open Food Facts public service to retrieve product information. Open Food Facts may receive the request and associated technical information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy practices. Successful results may be cached locally on your device.

See the Open Food Facts Privacy Policy.

Notifications

NutriLens can request notification permission to send optional meal and water reminders. Reminder schedules are stored locally and scheduled on your device. You can deny notification permission or turn reminders off in the app or your device settings.

How information is shared

NutriLens does not sell your personal information. Apart from the barcode lookup and the Google Play services model download described above, information stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it with another app or service.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall NutriLens. Exported files and backups are controlled by you and may remain wherever you save or share them.

Contact the developer if you have questions about this policy.

Children's privacy

NutriLens is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's practices change. The current version should be published at the privacy-policy URL linked from the Google Play listing and made available from within the app.
